About Lunaria

A studio built around patience.

Our story

It started with a bad facial, not a good one.

Lunaria opened in 2021 after our founder, Amaya, spent years booking treatments that promised results in an hour and delivered irritation instead. She trained under estheticians in Seoul and Colombo who worked the opposite way — slowly, and only after actually looking at the skin in front of them.

The studio is named for the Lunaria plant, whose seed pods turn silvery and translucent only after they've had time to dry undisturbed. That became the whole premise: nothing here is rushed, including you.

What we hold onto

A few things we don't compromise on.

No rushed reads

Every appointment opens with an unhurried skin reading in proper daylight — never a thirty-second glance before reaching for a product.

Small-batch, always

We mix our own serums and masks in-house, in batches small enough to adjust weekly as seasons and skin conditions shift.

Honest recommendations

If your skin doesn't need a treatment, we'll tell you — and suggest what it does need instead, even if that's simply time.
