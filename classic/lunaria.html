About us

Beauty, on a slower cycle.

"Lunaria" is named for the plant whose seed pods turn silvery and translucent as they dry — beauty that reveals itself gradually. We treat skin the same way: not as something to fix overnight, but something to tend, phase by phase.

Unhurried by design

No sixty-minute miracle facials. Our sessions run long because skin needs time to actually respond to a treatment, not just absorb it.

Formulated in-house

Our serums and masks are mixed in small batches at the studio, adjusted to the season and to what your skin is doing that week.

Read before treated

Every visit opens with a real skin reading under proper light — not a rushed glance — before we decide what the day's treatment should be.

The ritual

One appointment, four phases.

Every full treatment at Lunaria moves through the same arc — like the moon does.

Read

We start in proper light, no makeup, no assumptions — just what your skin is actually doing today.

Clear

Cleansing and exfoliation calibrated to what we found — never a fixed strength across every client.

Restore

Active serums and massage go in while the skin is most receptive, right after clearing.

Seal

A cold finish locks everything in, and you leave with a short plan for the next two weeks.
